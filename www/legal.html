4. Privacy Policy

Radio Prepper Network is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is handled.


4.1 Data stored only on your device

All of the following is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Stockpile items, quantities, and expiry dates
  • Household profile (number of people, calorie and water targets)
  • Checklist progress
  • Drill history and custom drills
  • Saved map pins and offline map tiles
  • Radio notebook entries
  • MeshCore network data
  • Logbook entries (unless you choose to share a contact live on the map)

4.2 Data collected if you create an RPN Community account

If you choose to create a free RPN Community account, the following data is stored on the RPN server:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and for account-related communications only
  • Password — stored as a secure hash; we never store or see your plain-text password
  • Display name and callsign — shown to other RPN members on the community map and contact list
  • Region and country — used to help members find others nearby
  • Radio equipment and active hours — shown on your public member profile if you choose to add them
  • Location data — only when you actively share your position or drop a community pin; not collected passively
  • Push notification token — used to deliver alerts from your local network; not linked to any advertising
  • Logbook contacts you choose to share live — visible to other members temporarily while active

4.3 Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

For users in the UK and European Economic Area, we process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to provide the community features you have signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — to maintain the security and integrity of the platform
  • Consent — for location sharing and live contact display, which you actively choose to enable

4.4 Data retention

Your community account data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, location data, and push token are deleted within 30 days. Logbook entries you shared live are removed immediately when the session ends.


4.5 Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers when you are online. This is subject to the OpenStreetMap usage policies. No personal data beyond a standard tile request is shared with OpenStreetMap.


4.6 Your rights

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.


4.7 Local data and backups

Your local app data (stockpile, checklists, drills, etc.) exists only on your device. We have no access to it and cannot recover it for you. Use More → Export Backup regularly to keep a copy safe.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

This app uses browser localStorage to save your data and preferences on your device. This is not the same as tracking cookies. No cookies are used for advertising or analytics purposes.


You can clear all locally stored app data at any time via your device settings (Settings → Apps → Field Brain → Storage → Clear Data). Note this will permanently delete all your local app data.
