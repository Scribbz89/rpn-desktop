What is Radio Prepper Network?

Radio Prepper Network (RPN) is a community built around practical preparedness, off-grid communications, and resilience. We believe that being prepared is not about fear — it is about responsibility. Responsibility to yourself, your family, and your community.


RPN covers the full spectrum of preparedness: emergency communications using amateur radio, CB, and mesh networking; food and water storage; first aid; and the practical skills needed to stay safe and functional when normal infrastructure fails.


We are based in Europe and focused on real-world, practical preparedness that works for everyday people — not survivalist fantasy.

What is RPN Field Brain?

Field Brain is RPN's offline-first emergency decision-support app. It is designed to answer four questions that matter when things go wrong:


  • What is happening? — Scenario Engine guides you through the situation step by step
  • What should I do? — Checklists and scenario actions give you clear, prioritised tasks
  • What resources do I have? — Stockpile tracks your food, water and supplies with expiry alerts
  • Who can help me? — Radio Notebook and Mesh Network store your contacts and frequencies

Everything works offline. No internet required. No accounts. No cloud. Your data stays on your device.
